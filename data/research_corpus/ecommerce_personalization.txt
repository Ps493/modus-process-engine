AI-driven product recommendation and personalisation is one of the more
measurable, revenue-linked AI applications in e-commerce, with multiple
independent industry analyses converging on broadly similar figures.
Retailers using AI-powered chat and recommendation systems report
conversion rates roughly four times higher than sites without AI
assistance (cited around 12.3% versus 3.1% in comparative studies), and
shoppers assisted by AI tools complete purchases faster. Personalisation
specifically is credited with driving a meaningful share of e-commerce
revenue directly — industry figures place product recommendations alone
at up to 31% of e-commerce revenue for retailers that have invested in
mature recommendation engines — and companies using AI personalisation
report roughly 40% higher revenue than those that do not, according to
compiled industry benchmarking. These gains are not without friction: the
same research notes a majority of consumers still report frustration with
impersonal shopping experiences even at companies using AI personalisation,
implying execution quality (relevance, not just presence of AI) drives the
gap between average and top-performing implementations. Site search
functionality shows a similar multiplier effect independent of
recommendations — site search users convert two to three times higher
than non-searchers — reinforcing that e-commerce AI investment concentrated
in search and recommendation surfaces tends to have clearer, more directly
attributable ROI than AI investment in less measurable areas like content
generation. Cart abandonment recovery, a related e-commerce process, is
also increasingly AI-assisted: personalised recommendations shown at the
point of abandonment are cited as reducing abandonment rates modestly but
measurably compared to generic re-engagement messaging.
