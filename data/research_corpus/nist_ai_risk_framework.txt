Organisations deploying AI systems, particularly those affecting employment, financial, or safety-related decisions, should implement governance processes covering data quality, bias testing, explainability, human oversight, and ongoing monitoring. Systems used in hiring, credit, and safety-critical operations warrant the highest level of scrutiny and human-in-the-loop design. Lower-risk applications such as internal reporting automation or inventory optimisation warrant proportionately lighter governance, but should still maintain audit trails and defined escalation paths for anomalous outputs.
